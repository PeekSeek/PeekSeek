Baseline, revised

Health stays the card's bottom edge, so the card is still exactly two lines tall. What changed: the notch now flares outward into the top edge, the loadout is one unbroken run with the powerup pushed to the far edge, headshots are a percentage, and the kill feed marks a headshot with a crosshair on an all-white weapon. Press Play round to judge the motion.

Format State

The notch is one continuous shape

Two <svg> fillets sit just outside the notch, flush to y = 0. Each is the notch colour minus a quarter disc centred on its outer top corner, so the fill is full width at the very top and tapers to nothing at depth r. That is the inverse of a rounded corner: the notch widens as it reaches the top edge and runs smoothly back into the straight line.

One run of items, one label

The divider is gone. Gun, kit and powerup items are a single left-to-right run, and the powerup name is pushed to the opposite edge by justify-content: space-between. The right column mirrors it with row-reverse, so the powerup always sits on the outside.

Nothing shouts

  • Powerup name uses the same --muted as ROUND 3, weight 400.
  • Name, K, D and HS% all share --card-text.
  • Headshots are round(hs / kills) as a percent, no HS.
  • Feed names are two plain white labels; weapons are forced white with brightness(0) invert(1).
