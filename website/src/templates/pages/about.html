About PeekSeek

PeekSeek is a free, open-source ELO matchmaking and esports platform for Unturned, created by Kieran van der Heijde (kvandeh). Sign in with Steam, join the queue alone or with a party, and the site puts you in a ranked match on a dedicated server against players of your level.

How ranked play works

Every player has an ELO rating. A win against a stronger player gains you more than a win against a weaker one. Your rating sets your rank on the ladder, from the first rank up to the highest. Each gamemode has its own queue.

Gamemodes

Arena: 1v1 and 2v2, with Sniper and Drums variants.
RAID: 1v1 and 2v2.
Before a match, players pick their kit, and in some modes they ban options.

Esports

PeekSeek runs cups and qualifiers for Unturned teams. Matches are played on the same servers as ranked play, and the results go on the site. Read the competition rulebook and the fair play rules, or follow the live competition.

Open source

The website, the Discord bot and the game server plugins are open source under the MIT licence. The queue and the rating system do not depend on Unturned, so the platform can run another game. PeekSeek source code on GitHub and the documentation show how it works.

Who made it
