Fair-Play Guidelines

At PeekSeek.net, our mission is to create a welcoming, competitive, and fair environment for all players. These guidelines outline the principles of fair play that ensure everyone can enjoy their time on the platform. Please adhere to these rules to maintain a respectful and balanced community.

1. Respect Other Players

1.1 Treat all participants with respect, regardless of skill level, background, or opinions.
1.2 Avoid using offensive, discriminatory, or toxic language in any communication, including in-game chat and forums.
1.3 Harassment, bullying, or targeting individuals for any reason will not be tolerated.

2. Honest Gameplay

2.1 Do not use cheats, bots, or unauthorized software to gain an unfair advantage.
2.2 Avoid exploiting bugs or glitches. If one is encountered, report it immediately and refrain from using it.
2.3 Transparency is crucial—play on your primary account and disclose if you have multiple accounts.

3. Sportsmanlike Behavior

3.1 Celebrate victories with humility and accept losses gracefully.
3.2 Rivalries are welcome, but any form of toxic or unsportsmanlike conduct is prohibited.
3.3 In-game communication (text, voice, or other methods) must remain respectful at all times.

4. Community Integrity

4.1 Protect your account by using secure passwords and enabling additional security measures.
4.2 Report any suspicious behavior or violations of these guidelines through the appropriate channels.
4.3 Contribute to a positive community by providing constructive feedback and supporting fellow players.

5. Compliance and Consequences

5.1 Follow all applicable laws and adhere to the PeekSeek.net Terms of Service.
5.2 Violations of these guidelines may result in penalties, including disqualification, account suspension, or a ban.
5.3 Decisions made by moderators or administrators regarding guideline enforcement are final.
