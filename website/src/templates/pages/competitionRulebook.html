Competition Rulebook

PeekSeek.net is a competitive network in which you can queue with players your skill level in games like Unturned. This page specifies all the rules that apply in PeekSeek events. These rules are not set to apply by default by any other party using PeekSeek.net to host their events, however they may still enforce the sames rules by themselves. Organisers may deviate from any of these rules to protect the competitive integrity of an event.

1. Steam accounts

1.1 Any Steam Account must be 3 months or older in order to be allowed into competition.
1.2 Usage of alternative accounts, that lead to an advantage, may lead to disqualification. This is left to the interpretation of the organizers.

2. Sportsmanlike Behavior

2.1 In-game chats (text, voice or any alternative method of communication) should not be used to negatively refer to one another.
2.2 Although rivalry is heavily supported, we prohibit any toxic behavoir towards your opponment through means of PeekSeek.net services. And this may lead to a disqualification.
2.3 You are not allowed to take part in competitions hosted for regions other than your own.

3. Cheating

3.1 Ping spoofing is considered a full violation and will lead to a disqualification and PeekSeek.net ban.
3.2 By playing, you agree not to use any external software which may lead to significant advantage within the game, such as: aimbot, wallhacks, radar hacks, etc. Software which changes the game visually or by routing is allowed, as long as it's not changed during the game (Crosshair overlay software and VPNs).
3.3 Glitching or using bugs is strictly prohibited and if triggered accidentally you are to immediently reverse the effects or let yourself die, the game will not be reset unless deemed neccessarry.
3.4 Matchfixing is the practice of predetermining the outcome of a match. This includes conspiring with others in advance to decide who will win, as well as knowingly participating in an event without the proper capabilities to compete fairly. Examples include entering with extremely high ping, participating without a stable internet connection, or failing to join the designated event server. This topic is not rigidly defined, and moderators may make slight exceptions to these rules in order to protect competitive integrity.
