Competitive matchmaking for Unturned

A non-profit ELO-based queue system built to level up the Unturned competitive scene—fair, fast, and community-driven.

Join the Queue

Personalize

Whether you use a Maplestrike with adaptive chambering or a Nightraider with a tactical laser, the choice is yours and our online kit editor will make everything easy for you.

We have 3 standard kit categories: Meta, Sniper and your Outfit. You can customize each however you like and you'll be able to choose if you want to use the Meta or Sniper kit depending on the gamemode.

Queue

Select your gamemode(s) and press queue, it's as simple as that. You'll be redirected once a match is found to select your kit and choose any decisions based on movement and or bullet drop. You will only be matched with people in your skill group.

Your match will then be given to a server where you can play on until it's done.

Compete

PeekSeek is more than a queue system, we host the biggest Unturned tournaments every season. We fund the UCL (Unturned Competitive League) which hosts tournaments like never before.

Think you can compete? Join our Discord to learn more.

Our Mission

PeekSeek exists to strengthen the Unturned competitive scene by offering fair, accessible matchmaking to everyone—free from paywalls, bias, or toxicity. We're built by players, for players. Everything we do is to improve competition, empower communities, and grow the game we love. PeekSeek is a non-profit, 100% of our earnings go towards tournament prize money.
