# Code of conduct

This is a competitive shooter's codebase. The standard in here is lower-drama than the
standard in a match, not higher.

## The rules

**Talk about the code.** Review a change, not the person who wrote it. "This drops the ban
on a retry" is useful; "did you even test this" is not, and means the same thing worse.

**Assume the other person had a reason.** Most of what looks wrong in a diff is a
constraint you cannot see yet. Ask what it was.

**Take the loss.** Sometimes your approach is not the one that ships. Say so and move on.
Re-litigating a settled decision in a new thread wastes everyone's evening.

**Keep the game out of it.** Whatever happened in a match, in a queue or in a Discord
channel does not come into a pull request, and a contributor's rank is not an argument.

## Not allowed

Harassment, in the issues or the PRs or anywhere this project reaches. Slurs. Sexual
attention nobody asked for. Going after someone's account, address or identity, or
threatening to. Publishing a vulnerability before it is fixed to make a point. Doxxing, in
either direction, for any reason.

Being consistently unpleasant counts too, even when every individual message would pass.

## If something happens

Report it to **peekseek@kcvdh.com**, or to any maintainer directly. Say what happened
and where; a link is enough. Reports are handled privately and we will not name you to the
person involved without asking you first.

What happens next is a judgement call and we will make it: usually a private word, since
most of this is someone having a bad week. Beyond that, a public one, then losing access to
the repository, the Discord, or the platform. Severity and whether it has happened before
both matter. Retaliating against someone for reporting is treated as the original thing,
worse.

A report made in bad faith to get at somebody is itself a breach.

## Scope

This repository, its issues and pull requests, the project's Discord, and anywhere someone
is representing PeekSeek. It is not a licence to police what contributors do elsewhere,
and it is not void because something started elsewhere.
