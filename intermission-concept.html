Intermission screen

What used to fill the gap before a round actually starts. A Valorant-style agent-select beat: both rosters laid out side by side over av.png, so viewers can read who is playing, what they picked, and what they are up against — instead of watching "Starting soon / Loading teams…" sit on screen with nothing to look at.

Where this sits: replaces #notStarted's current bare splash on overlay.html, for the selectKitsTime window between a match being found and the first round actually starting. Data it needs that the live snapshot doesn't send today: each player's chosen kit and powerup while picks are still being made — /match/overlay already has this in params.userData, it just isn't rendered here yet. Mock scenario: Casual Duo (two players per team, per mmconfig.json) rather than the 1v1 Competitive shown in the first draft — a lone player next to a lone "team name" didn't actually exercise the roster layout this screen exists for.
